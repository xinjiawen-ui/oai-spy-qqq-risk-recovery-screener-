Scoring methodology

Daily closing prices from Yahoo Finance through yfinance. Higher fragility means more observed stress; higher recovery means more positive evidence. Neither score is a calibrated probability. Unavailable inputs reduce coverage.

Qualitative risk dashboard

Five 1–5 descriptive categories use available binary market-stress conditions. Each rating equals round(1 + 4 × triggered conditions / observed conditions). Categories: semiconductor and technology leadership; equal-weight breadth and down days; Treasury and credit ETF proxies; VIX/VVIX and realized volatility; technology versus staples leadership. Missing conditions are omitted; categories with no observations show N/A. Not independent macro news, institutional flow data, or calibrated probabilities.

Fragility (100-point binary model)

Breadth: RSP/SPY below EMA20 (15), negative 5-day change (10). Price: below EMA20 (10), below EMA50 (5), volume >1.25× 20-day average while below EMA20 (5). Leadership: SOXX/QQQ below EMA20 (10), XLK/XLP below EMA20 (5). VIX: five-day increase ≥15% (5), VIX/EMA20 >1.15 (5), VIX/VIX3M ≥1 (5). Credit: HYG/LQD below EMA20 (10), negative five-day change (5). Rates: IEF five-day price return ≤−1% (10). Total points are not normalized for missing observations.

Recovery (100-point binary model)

RSP/SPY improving over five days (15), above EMA20 (10); SOXX/QQQ above EMA20 (15), XLK/XLP above EMA20 (5); HYG/LQD improving over five days (12), above EMA20 (8); positive five-day index return (10), positive daily return with above-average volume (5); falling VIX over five days (5), VIX/VIX3M below 1 (5); index above EMA20 (10). Recovery is independent of fragility, not its inverse.

Early deleveraging warning (100-point normalized model)

At least two weak participation ratios among IWM/SPY, XLY/XLP, XLK/XLU (20); 5d/20d realized-volatility ratio ≥1.3 (15); VVIX/EMA20 ≥1.1 (10); VIX/VIX3M ≥0.95 (10); down-day volume share ≥60% over ten days (15); at least seven down days out of ten (10); falling HYG/LQD over five days (15); 63-session drawdown ≤−4% (5). Score = 100 × triggered available weight / available weight. At least 80% coverage required. Bands: <30 Low, 30–49 Monitor, 50–69 Reduce Exposure Watch, ≥70 Deleverage Warning.

Recovery confirmation (six conditions)

Positive five-day RSP/SPY change; positive five-day HYG/LQD change; SOXX/QQQ above EMA20; positive five-day index return; negative five-day VIX change; at most one weak risk-on participation ratio. At least five observations required. Confirmed Re-risk Watch: ≥5 confirmations, recovery ≥60, warning <50, warning coverage ≥80. Recovery Developing: ≥4 confirmations and recovery ≥50. Two or three: Unconfirmed Bounce.

Early rebound radar (eight conditions)

Index within 1.5% of prior ten-day low; RSI(2) below 15; HYG/LQD, RSP/SPY or SOXX/QQQ each improving over three days while index three-day return is nonpositive; VIX or VVIX each declining over three days while index remains weak; and a positive daily index return. Four or more hits: Early Reversal Watch; two or three: Potential Stabilization. Fewer than six available: Insufficient Data. The last condition is confirmation, not a leading signal.

Enhanced v2.1 groups these signals into exhaustion, divergences, volatility easing and price follow-through. The early score weights these groups 20%, 35%, 25% and 20% respectively, normalized for observed data. Recovery confirmation additionally requires improving credit and breadth and positive three-day price momentum, with at least 70% coverage. All ratings are descriptive and have not been walk-forward calibrated.

Legacy five-factor model and entry signal

One point each for index below EMA20 or EMA20 below EMA50; VIX ≥25; VIX/VIX3M ≥1; HYG/LQD below EMA20; XLK/XLP below EMA20. Entry requires a fresh move to ≥4 flags, RSI(2) below 35 and HYG/LQD credit filter PASS. It is not a validated bottom prediction.

Metric dictionary

Breadth and leadership

RSP/SPY: equal-weight versus cap-weight S&P 500 proxy, not actual constituent breadth. IWM/SPY: small-cap relative strength. XLY/XLP: discretionary versus staples. XLK/XLU: tech versus utilities. XLK/XLP: technology versus staples. SOXX/QQQ: semiconductors versus Nasdaq-100. Positive five-day ratio change or ratio above EMA20 indicates relative improvement, not necessarily an absolute price gain.

Volatility and tail-risk proxies

VIX: S&P 500 option-implied volatility. VIX3M: three-month implied volatility; VIX/VIX3M above one indicates backwardation. VVIX: implied volatility of VIX options. VVIX/EMA20: volatility-of-volatility relative to its twenty-day trend. Realized vol 20d: annualized standard deviation of daily returns. Volatility acceleration: five-day standard deviation divided by twenty-day standard deviation. These are proxies, not dealer gamma or actual forced liquidations.

Credit, rates and selling pressure

HYG/LQD: high-yield versus investment-grade bond ETF ratio; HYG/SHY: high-yield versus short Treasury ETF ratio. IEF: intermediate Treasury ETF price, not a direct yield. Down-volume share: volume on ETF down days divided by total ETF volume over ten sessions, not exchange-wide declining volume. Negative days (10): number of down-return sessions. 63d drawdown: close versus rolling 63-session closing high.

Technical indicators, sizing and coverage

EMA20 and EMA50: exponential moving averages. RSI(2): two-session short-term oscillator; RSI(14): Wilder-smoothed fourteen-session oscillator. Volume ratio: today's ETF volume / twenty-session average. Core exposure: min(100%, target annualized volatility / twenty-session realized volatility), using SPY 15% and QQQ 20% targets. Coverage: observed weight out of possible weight. Signals are end-of-day; earliest hypothetical execution is next session. Historical scores, trade studies and their limitations remain in Backtests & history.
